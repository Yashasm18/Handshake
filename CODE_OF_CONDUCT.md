# Code of Conduct

Handshake exists to make deals between strangers safer. We want working on it to feel the same way: respectful, honest and welcoming to everyone, whatever their background, identity, experience or level of skill.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to open source.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Give and take feedback about the work, never about the person.
- Credit other people's ideas and contributions.
- Keep discussions focused on what's best for the people who use Handshake.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, intimidation or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Insulting, discriminatory or sexual language or imagery.
- Sharing someone's private information, such as an address, email or PayPal details, without their permission.
- Posting real people's chats, deals or scam reports in issues or pull requests. Use made-up examples instead.
- Any other behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

In every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If something here is broken, tell us privately: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, mentioning that it's a conduct report (the same private channel as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential.

## What happens next

We'll listen, look into it fairly, and act in proportion: from a private word, to a public warning, to a temporary or permanent ban from the project. People who report in good faith will never be penalised for it.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
